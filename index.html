About Me

I am a researcher with an interdisciplinary background in food science and biotechnology. I hold an MSc in Food Science and Biotechnology with Distinction and a First-Class BSc (Hons) in Biotechnology.

My research experience spans sustainable mushroom cultivation, circular bioresources, electrochemical biosensors, nanobiotechnology, microbiology and analytical food science. I am particularly interested in translating biotechnology into solutions for sustainable food production, nutrition and health.

Research

🍄 Sustainable Mushroom Cultivation

Investigating the valorisation of brewers' spent grain as a sustainable substrate for Pleurotus ostreatus cultivation and post-harvest quality.

🧬 Biosensors & Diagnostics

Development of 3D-printed graphene-polymer electrochemical biosensors for biomarker detection.

🌊 Circular Food Biotechnology

Exploring seaweed, brewing side-streams and other bioresources for sustainable food and biotechnology applications.

🧪 Nanobiotechnology

Green synthesis and characterisation of metal nanoparticles for antioxidant, antibacterial and photocatalytic applications.
