About Me

I am a researcher with an interdisciplinary background in food science and biotechnology. I hold an MSc in Food Science and Biotechnology with Distinction and a First-Class BSc (Hons) in Biotechnology.

My research experience spans sustainable mushroom cultivation, circular bioresources, electrochemical biosensors, nanobiotechnology, microbiology and analytical food science. I am particularly interested in translating biotechnology into solutions for sustainable food production, nutrition and health.

RESEARCH

Selected Research

My research spans food biotechnology, sustainable bioresources, biosensor development and nanobiotechnology, with an emphasis on translating laboratory science into applications in food, sustainability and human health.

01

Sustainable Food Biotechnology

Brewers' Spent Grain for Oyster Mushroom Cultivation

Investigating the valorisation of brewers' spent grain as a nutrient-rich substrate for the cultivation of Pleurotus ostreatus, with evaluation of mycelial growth, yield, biological efficiency and post-harvest quality.

Key finding: A 30% BSG formulation produced the highest fresh yield and biological efficiency among the tested commercial substrate formulations.
Mushroom cultivation Circular bioeconomy Food waste valorisation Texture analysis
02

Biosensors & Diagnostics

PAGEMS: 3D-Printed Electrochemical Biosensor

Contributing to the development of a 3D-printed graphene-polymer electrochemical biosensor for the detection of prostate-specific antigen as a potential platform for early prostate cancer diagnostics.

Research focus: Electrode activation, surface modification, electrochemical characterisation and antibody-based biosensor functionalisation.
3D printing Graphene-PLA CV / EIS / SWV Biosensors
03

Circular Bioresources

Seaweed and Brewers' Spent Grain Valorisation

Exploring the use of seaweed and brewing side-streams as sustainable biological resources, including biomass characterisation and their incorporation into novel cultivation substrates.

Research focus: Biomass preparation, compositional characterisation and sustainable substrate development.
Seaweed BSG Biomass Sustainable food systems
04

Nanobiotechnology

Green Synthesis of Silver Nanoparticles

Investigating plant-mediated synthesis of silver nanoparticles using Caesalpinia pulcherrima flower extracts and evaluating their physicochemical and biological properties.

Applications investigated: Antioxidant, antibacterial and photocatalytic activity.
Silver nanoparticles Green synthesis UV-Vis FTIR
